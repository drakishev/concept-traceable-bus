How to fill in this sheet

  • For each report, record what the text states for every item. Do not judge whether it is medically right.
  • Choose not stated if the report does not mention the item, or if it is unreadable.
  • A report may be garbled or repeat itself: still record what it states, or choose not stated if the item is unreadable.
  • The report wording differs from the buttons: choose the button that means the same thing.
  • Work on your own, without AI tools, and do not discuss the reports with the other reader.
  • Answers are saved in this browser as you click. When you have finished, press Download CSV and send the file back.

Keyboard: number keys choose an option in the highlighted row (0 = not stated), ↑/↓ move between rows, ←/→ or Enter move between reports.
